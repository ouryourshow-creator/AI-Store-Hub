# Universal AI Prompt

Use this prompt as the base for almost every Keytopia visual:

"Create a premium minimalist technology scene for the Keytopia brand. Use a white and deep navy color palette with subtle electric blue accents. Include soft ambient lighting, rounded UI elements, floating glass panels, premium workspace aesthetics, modern AI inspired interfaces, clean geometry, high contrast, generous whitespace, realistic reflections, soft shadows, Apple and Stripe inspired design language, ultra detailed, photorealistic, cinematic lighting, 8K quality. Leave negative space for product placement and marketing text."

---

# Design Principles

Every design must pass these five tests:

1. Can someone recognize it as Keytopia without reading the logo?
2. Does it look trustworthy?
3. Does it feel premium?
4. Is there enough whitespace?
5. Would Apple or Stripe approve this layout?

If any answer is "No," simplify the design.

---

# Brand Rules

Always prioritize clarity over decoration.

Use one focal point per design.

Keep messaging concise.

Never overcrowd layouts.

Every visual should communicate trust before price.

## Future Roadmap

As Keytopia grows, the identity can expand into a complete ecosystem with a website, mobile app, loyalty program, animated brand mascot based on the "K" icon, custom illustrations, branded packaging for gift cards, and a consistent motion system across ads and social media.

The goal is for someone to recognize a Keytopia design instantly, even before they see the logo.
